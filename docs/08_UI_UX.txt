15. Branding Tokens
Do not hard-code arbitrary colors throughout the app.
Use design tokens.
Typography
Recommended:
UI sans: Inter, Geist, or system sans stack
Code: JetBrains Mono or system monospace
Default recommendation:
--font-sans: Inter, ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif;
--font-mono: "JetBrains Mono", ui-monospace, monospace;
Color Strategy
Use semantic roles:
background
surface
surface-muted
text-primary
text-secondary
border
brand
success
warning
danger
info
focus
Do not make trust/verification depend on color alone.
Spacing
Use a consistent 4px or 8px scale.
Radius
Use restrained rounded corners; avoid making every element pill-shaped.
16. Responsive Breakpoints
Mobile-first.
Suggested design breakpoints:
Small: ~640px
Medium: ~768px
Large: ~1024px
XL: ~1280px
These are design guidance, not hard business rules.

21. UX Anti-Patterns
Do not:
Force signup before browsing
Force signup before seeing demo
Hide fees until the last step later
Auto-enroll users into paid plans
Sell verification
Rank solely by payment
Rank solely by likes
Expose private email
Make demo autoplay with sound
Use endless infinite scroll everywhere
Make clients submit 20 fields before first inquiry
