TRUST, SAFETY & MODERATION
1. Purpose
Trust is a product feature. This document governs user-generated product claims, external links, client inquiries, reviews, IP complaints, fraud, spam, and enforcement.
2. Safety Principles
Restrict high-risk content quickly when credible harm is possible.
Preserve evidence and audit actions.
Give users understandable reasons for enforcement.
Trust labels must be precise.
Do not sell trust or positive reviews.
Protect both developers from fake clients and clients from fake developers.
Provide an appeal path for meaningful enforcement.
3. Content Categories
Allowed
genuine products;
client-safe case studies;
open-source work;
learning projects clearly labeled;
private/NDA-safe descriptions with permission.
Restricted/Prohibited
stolen work;
impersonation;
malicious/phishing links;
malware distribution;
confidential client data without permission;
fraudulent testimonials;
spam;
harassment/abuse;
illegal services/content;
manipulated reviews;
deceptive verification claims.
4. Product Review Checklist
Admin checks:
product description consistency;
proof relevance;
live URL safety/reachability;
contributor claim;
business category;
technology tags;
obvious IP misuse;
confidential data exposure;
project-type honesty;
quality threshold.
Learning/tutorial projects may be allowed but must be labeled and should not outrank strong commercial proof solely through engagement.

6. Severity Levels
Critical
Examples:
active phishing/malware;
credential theft;
severe privacy exposure.
Action:
immediate restriction;
security investigation;
account suspension where justified.
High
Examples:
credible stolen commercial work;
impersonation;
repeated fake client leads.
Medium
Examples:
misleading project labeling;
repeated low-quality spam.
Low
Examples:
minor metadata/category problems.
7. Enforcement Actions
No Action
Request Changes
Warning
Remove Link
Unpublish Product
Temporary Restriction
Suspend Account
Permanent Ban
Reason is mandatory for meaningful enforcement.
8. Moderation Workflow
Report/Detection
→ Open
→ Triage
→ Evidence Review
→ No Action / Warning / Restriction / Suspension
→ Notify affected user where appropriate
→ Appeal opportunity
→ Resolve/Close
Critical safety events may be restricted before full review.
9. Appeals
Users may appeal:
product rejection;
suspension;
review removal;
IP enforcement where appropriate.
Appeal must be reviewed by someone other than the original moderator when team size permits.
Store:
original decision;
evidence;
appeal statement;
appeal result.
10. Intellectual Property / Copyright
Maintain a formal takedown process.
Evidence may include:
ownership URL;
repository history;
client authorization;
original files;
legal notice.
Do not require public disclosure of confidential source code merely to resolve a claim.
11. Client Confidentiality
If a product exposes:
private credentials;
personal data;
confidential company information;
internal admin URLs;
the platform may immediately unpublish while investigating.
12. Malicious External Links
Controls:
safe URL validation;
domain/reputation checks where available;
user reports;
periodic reachability checks later;
remove/disable unsafe link without necessarily deleting entire case study.
A malicious-link finding may trigger account-level review.
13. Fake Developer Prevention
Initial controls:
email verification;
manual profile/product review;
contribution requirement;
external proof;
duplicate/stolen-content reports.
Later:
identity verification;
GitHub contribution verification;
client confirmation;
domain ownership verification.
14. Fake Client / Inquiry Abuse
Signals:
unverified email;
mass identical inquiries;
suspicious domains;
repeated no-response behavior;
abusive content;
impossible budget/promises;
developer reports.
Controls:
confirmation;
throttling;
quarantine;
block;
client reputation later.
Spam leads are excluded from developer response metrics.
15. Review Integrity
Rules:
verified completion required;
no review purchase;
no reciprocal fake-review rings;
no self-review;
one review per engagement;
platform may remove confidential/abusive text while preserving legitimate rating/outcome where policy permits.

17. Ranking Safety
Do not let:
paid plan;
upvotes;
friend traffic;
spam saves
alone determine ranking.
Apply fraud detection and minimum quality gates before using engagement signals.
18. New Builder Fairness
Reserve reasonable discovery opportunities for:
newly approved;
high-quality;
relevant;
available
builders.
This is not random exposure; quality/relevance remain required.
19. Team/Studio Safety — V2
Team creation limited to approved builders.
Role/member changes are audited.
Contributions are explicit.
Leaving a team does not erase historical contribution.
Team cannot misrepresent temporary contractors as permanent employees.
20. Moderation SLAs
Do not publish unrealistic promises initially.
Internal priorities:
Critical safety: immediate/urgent triage
High: same business day where feasible
Medium: queue-based
Low: normal review queue
Adjust with staffing and legal obligations.
21. Transparency
Maintain internal metrics:
reports received;
report categories;
action rate;
appeals;
reversal rate;
malicious-link incidents;
IP complaints.
Public transparency reporting may be added as scale/legal requirements justify it.
22. Moderator Access
Moderators see only information needed for their task.
Sensitive/private inquiry access should be limited, logged, and justified.
23. Evidence Retention
Define retention for:
abuse reports;
IP claims;
moderation decisions;
security incidents.
Retention must balance legal defense, safety, and privacy minimization.
24. Launch Gate
Before public launch:
report button works;
moderation queue works;
suspend/unpublish works;
malicious URL escalation works;
copyright contact/process exists;
reasons/appeals are documented;
admin actions are audited.
