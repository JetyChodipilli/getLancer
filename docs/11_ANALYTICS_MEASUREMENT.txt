4. Event Envelope
Every analytics event should support:
{
  "eventId":"uuid",
  "eventName":"product_view",
  "occurredAt":"2026-09-04T10:00:00Z",
  "anonymousId":"anon_...",
  "userId":null,
  "sessionId":"sess_...",
  "source":"web",
  "properties":{}
}
Do not place confidential inquiry text, passwords, tokens, private case-study contents, or raw sensitive identifiers in analytics properties.

8. Marketplace Liquidity Indicators
Important:
% client searches that produce >=3 relevant active products;
% qualified inquiries that receive response;
time to first developer response;
% unavailable-builder pages with viable fallback candidates later;
concentration of inquiries among top 10% of developers.
High concentration may indicate ranking/new-builder discovery problems.
9. Showcase Slot Analytics
Measure:
active slot utilization;
archive/reactivate frequency;
time to first 3 active showcases;
developers blocked by slot limit;
conversion difference between 1/2/3 active showcase users;
later, earned unlock vs paid upgrade behavior.
Do not optimize slot policy only for paid conversion; preserve marketplace quality.
10. Acquisition Attribution
Track first-touch and last meaningful touch where practical:
organic search;
LinkedIn/social;
referral;
direct;
developer-shared product;
founder outreach;
agency referral;
paid acquisition later.
The key acquisition metric is not visitor volume but qualified inquiry and confirmed hire by source.

12. Privacy Rules
Respect consent for non-essential analytics where required.
Pseudonymize anonymous IDs.
Avoid sending client project descriptions to third-party analytics.
Avoid developer/client email in analytics payloads.
Define retention.
Provide deletion handling where required.
13. Experimentation
Do not launch large A/B infrastructure initially.
When needed, experiments must define:
hypothesis;
primary metric;
guardrail metrics;
duration/sample rationale;
assignment key;
success threshold.
Never experiment on security, legal consent, or trust labels in misleading ways.

15. Data Quality Rules
Server-authoritative events for business outcomes.
Deduplicate by event ID/idempotency key.
UTC timestamps.
Document event versioning.
Monitor event drop/spike anomalies.
Do not silently rename events without migration/dashboard update.
16. Early Validation Scorecard
Target cohort:
20 curated builders;
~50–60 active high-quality showcases;
5–10 qualified inquiries;
2–5 serious discussions;
1–3 paid engagements.
These are validation targets, not permanent growth benchmarks.
17. What Not to Optimize
Do not make primary success metrics:
raw registrations;
follower counts;
comments;
likes/upvotes;
pageviews without client intent.
The product must remain commercially outcome-oriented.
