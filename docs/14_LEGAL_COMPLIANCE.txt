LEGAL & COMPLIANCE
Important: This document is product/legal-requirements guidance, not legal advice. Obtain qualified legal review before public launch, international expansion, payments, source-code sales, or enterprise contracts.
1. V1 Legal Position
V1 is primarily:
A proof-of-work publishing platform
A developer discovery service
A lead-generation marketplace
V1 is not yet:
An escrow provider
A payment processor
A guarantor of project completion
A source-code reseller
An employer of listed developers
A party to every external freelance contract
Terms must reflect the actual product.

8.1 Additional Global Marketplace Review
Because the platform hosts user-generated profiles, product media, and external links, obtain jurisdiction-specific legal review for at least:
European Union: Digital Services Act obligations that may apply to hosting/online-platform services, including notice-and-action and transparency duties.
United States: DMCA safe-harbor requirements, including designated-agent and notice/takedown procedures where applicable.
India: Digital Personal Data Protection Act and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025 for personal-data processing.
India: Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, as amended, for intermediary/user-generated-content obligations where applicable.
Do not treat this list as exhaustive; launch jurisdictions and business model determine actual obligations.

"Secure Code"
