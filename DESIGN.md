## Motion and responsive behavior
Use shared 260ms feedback and 600ms settling transitions with cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1). The requested hero motion includes 950–1100ms staggered entrances, 7–8.5s seven-pixel floating cycles, and frame-rate-independent pointer easing with a maximum six-pixel horizontal/four-pixel vertical shift. Cards lift eight pixels on hover; keyboard focus has equivalent emphasis. Ambient motion pauses offscreen, when the tab is hidden, and while a hero control has focus. Touch devices do not get pointer parallax. Reduced motion disables all hero animations, transitions and smooth scrolling. Animate transform/opacity, with no displacement shader or continuous JavaScript loop at rest. Optical refraction is pre-rendered in the decorative asset; backdrop blur is restrained to chrome. Respect reduced motion and reduced transparency; opaque fallbacks cover unsupported blur and forced colors.

At 1000px simplify navigation/workspace rail; at 760px use the menu and stack search; at 640px use one-column forms and cards. Hero scene reserves its dimensions and remains within the viewport at 320px. At 200% text zoom headings wrap and controls remain reachable.
